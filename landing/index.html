FROM SIGNAL TO UNDERSTANDING

Every run tells a story.
See the whole picture.

A score is a starting point. Connect the numbers to the page, the history, and the technical work that moves your site forward.

◎

A workspace for your web.

Organize production, staging, and signed-in pages into modules. Run one website or an entire project, and follow each audit as it finishes.

  • Four supported Lighthouse scoring categories
  • Saved authentication for authorized websites
  • Visible progress, failures, and captured pages
⌕

Go beyond the headline score.

Open any run from your searchable audit history. Compare it with the previous result, inspect the captured page, and read the original Lighthouse findings.

Savings appear only when Lighthouse provides an estimate. Full JSON reports remain available for deeper investigation.

◷

Measured progress.

Compare category scores across real runs. Find regressions with exact timestamps and previous-run changes.

⌕

Know what to fix.

Explore Lighthouse findings by category. See estimated savings when supplied, then inspect the full diagnostic report.

▣

Keep the evidence.

A screenshot, the report, and your history stay together locally. Return to the details whenever you need them.

◇

Your work stays yours.

SQLite stores your audit history on your device. Desktop passwords use OS-backed protection when available.

A SMALL ROUTINE. A CLEARER PICTURE.

From URL to understanding.

Use the same repeatable workflow every time you ship.

  1. Add your websites

    Group related pages into a module.

  2. Configure access

    Add login details if a page requires them.

  3. Run a local audit

    Chrome and Lighthouse do the measuring.

  4. Investigate findings

    Review scores, screenshots, and diagnostics.

  5. Track your progress

    Compare the next run with your history.

BUILT FOR THE PEOPLE WHO BUILD THE WEB

A focused tool.
An open foundation.

Lighthouse for the measurements. Electron for the desktop. React for the workspace. Node.js and SQLite for the local engine.

No hosted audit service. No required cloud account. The public website introduces the product; the desktop app runs your audits on your machine.
